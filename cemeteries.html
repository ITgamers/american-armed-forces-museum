Cemeteries

Otero County Cemetery Records

Our research library maintains burial records for the following Otero County cemeteries, with an emphasis on veteran burials.

  • Alamogordo — Monte Vista Cemetery
  • Avis
  • Blazer's Mill
  • Boles Acres
  • James Canyon
  • Our Lady of Guadalupe
  • Mayhill
  • Sixteen Springs Canyon
  • Three Rivers
  • Timberon Memorial Park
  • Tularosa — Fairview Cemetery

Looking for a specific record? Contact the Museum and our research volunteers will help you search.
